Comparative Analysisતુલનાત્મક વિશ્લેષણ

Five head-to-head comparisons that shaped the design decisions in this pipeline.

Comparison

Retrieval QA vs. Generative QAપુનઃપ્રાપ્તિ vs. જનરેટિવ

AspectRetrieval QAGenerative QA
WorkingExtracts an exact span from source textGenerates a new sentence conditioned on retrieved context
ComplexityLower — no generation model neededHigher — needs a language model plus retrieval
AdvantagesGrounded, fast, hard to hallucinateFluent, can synthesize across multiple passages
DisadvantagesAnswer must exist verbatim in sourceCan hallucinate if retrieval is weak
Use casesFAQ lookup, form-field extractionConversational assistants, document chatbots
Comparison

BERT vs. mBERTBERT vs. mBERT

AspectBERTmBERT
WorkingContextual encoder trained on English textSame architecture trained jointly on 100+ languages
ComplexitySingle-language vocabularyShared multilingual vocabulary, larger training set
AdvantagesBest-in-class for English tasksOne model covers many languages, including Gujarati
DisadvantagesNo support for non-English scriptsSlightly lower per-language accuracy than a dedicated model
Use casesEnglish extractive QA, classificationCross-lingual and bilingual QA
Comparison

Word2Vec vs. FastTextWord2Vec vs. FastText

AspectWord2VecFastText
WorkingLearns one vector per whole wordLearns vectors from character n-grams, then sums per word
ComplexitySimpler, smaller modelSlightly heavier due to subword vectors
AdvantagesFast to train, well understoodHandles rare and misspelled words gracefully
DisadvantagesCannot represent out-of-vocabulary wordsSlightly larger memory footprint
Use casesGeneral word-similarity tasksMorphologically rich languages like Gujarati
Comparison

English QA vs. Gujarati QAઅંગ્રેજી vs. ગુજરાતી QA

AspectEnglish QAGujarati QA
WorkingDirect use of mature English NLP toolingRequires Unicode normalization and script-aware models
ComplexityLower — abundant pretrained resourcesHigher — smaller pretrained ecosystem
AdvantagesHigh accuracy out of the boxExtends access to 55M+ Gujarati speakers
DisadvantagesExcludes non-English speakersFewer benchmarks to validate against
Use casesGlobal-facing productsRegional education and citizen services
Comparison

TF-IDF vs. EmbeddingsTF-IDF vs. એમ્બેડિંગ

AspectTF-IDFEmbeddings
WorkingWeighs words by frequency and rarityRepresents meaning as a dense vector
ComplexitySimple, no training requiredRequires a pretrained or trained encoder
AdvantagesFast, interpretable, no GPU neededCaptures semantic similarity, handles synonyms
DisadvantagesBlind to meaning — exact words must matchNeeds more compute and a trained model
Use casesLightweight keyword searchSemantic search, RAG retrieval
