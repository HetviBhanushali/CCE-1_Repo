Domain use

Where This System Fits

Education

Concepts used: Text preprocessing, tokenization, TF-IDF / embeddings, retrieval QA

Why it's needed: Students often need an answer buried inside a textbook chapter or lecture PDF, not the whole document.

Benefits: Faster doubt-resolution, self-paced learning, works in the student's own language.

Customer Support

Concepts used: Tokenization, word embeddings, language models, RAG

Why it's needed: Support teams field the same questions repeatedly across product manuals and policy documents.

Benefits: Instant, consistent answers around the clock; reduces ticket load on human agents.

Government Citizen Services

Concepts used: Gujarati preprocessing pipeline, tokenization, multilingual (mBERT) embeddings

Why it's needed: Citizens interacting with government schemes and forms often prefer their regional language over English.

Benefits: Wider reach, reduced dependency on intermediaries, more equitable access to public services.

Healthcare Information

Concepts used: Text preprocessing, stop-word removal, extractive QA, retrieval

Why it's needed: Patients need quick, accurate answers from medical leaflets and health advisories, in a language they trust.

Benefits: Improves health literacy, reduces miscommunication, supports rural clinics with limited staff.

Banking

Concepts used: Tokenization, word embeddings, language models, intent-aware retrieval

Why it's needed: Customers ask policy and procedure questions that live inside dense terms-and-conditions documents.

Benefits: Reduces branch and call-centre load, gives consistent answers across channels.

Legal Document Search (optional)

Concepts used: TF-IDF, vector search (FAISS), retrieval QA

Why it's needed: Legal documents are long, dense, and precise wording matters — exactly what retrieval-based QA is built for.

Benefits: Cuts research time, surfaces the exact clause instead of the whole contract.
