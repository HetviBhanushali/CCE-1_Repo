01

Why Question Answering Systems?

Search engines return documents; a Question Answering (QA) system returns the answer itself, pulled or generated from those documents. That shift — from "here are ten links" to "here is the sentence you needed" — is what makes QA the interface layer for education platforms, government helplines, customer support desks, and voice assistants. It matters even more in a multilingual country like India, where the person asking and the document holding the answer may not share a script.

02

English + Gujarati Support

Why Gujarati specifically

Gujarati is spoken by over 55 million people, yet most public NLP tooling, benchmarks, and pretrained models are English-first. Building QA support for Gujarati means handling Unicode normalization, a distinct script, and far smaller training corpora.

What "bilingual" means here

Not two separate systems bolted together — one shared retrieval-and-reasoning pipeline with language-aware preprocessing at the front and script-aware generation at the back, detailed on the Workflow page.
