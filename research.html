The ideas and tools this project builds on, grouped by where they come from.

Academic

Recent Researchતાજેતરનું સંશોધન

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Pairing a retriever with a generator lets language models answer from a document they were never trained on, grounding responses in retrieved evidence instead of memorized parameters.

Multilingual Large Language Models

Models trained across dozens of languages at once show that cross-lingual transfer can lift performance on lower-resource languages like Gujarati, even without proportionally large Gujarati training data.

Industry

Industry Applicationsઉદ્યોગ ઉપયોગો

Customer Support Automation

Enterprises deploy RAG-based assistants over their own documentation to deflect repetitive support tickets before they reach a human agent.

Enterprise Knowledge Assistants

Internal QA systems index company wikis, policies, and reports so employees can ask questions in plain language instead of searching folder trees.

Tooling

Open Source Frameworksઓપન સોર્સ ફ્રેમવર્ક

Hugging Face

Hosts pretrained transformer models and tokenizers, including multilingual encoders usable for Gujarati.

LangChain

Provides the orchestration layer connecting document loaders, retrievers, and language models into a single RAG pipeline.

FAISS

Handles the nearest-neighbour vector search step that retrieval depends on, at scale.
